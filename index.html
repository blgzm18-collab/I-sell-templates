About the Server

We focus on meaningful conversation, creative collaboration, and regular events that bring people together.

Who we are

Creators, devs, and players who value respectful discussion and helpful feedback.

What we do

Weekly events, mentorship channels, project showcases, and casual voice hangouts.

Why join

Grow your network, find collaborators, and enjoy a moderated, friendly space.

Featured Channels

#announcements

Official updates, event schedules, and server news.

#collab

Find teammates, post project ideas, and recruit contributors.

#showcase

Share your work and get constructive feedback.

#voice-lounge

Drop in for casual chats, co-working, or game nights.

Community Rules

  1. Be respectful and inclusive.
  2. No harassment, hate speech, or spam.
  3. Keep channels on-topic and use threads for long discussions.
  4. Follow moderator instructions.
